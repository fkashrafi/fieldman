    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

    Do I need RO, or is a filter enough?

    It depends on TDS. Below about 500 mg/L, filtration plus UV is usually enough. Above 1,000 mg/L, common for Karachi borewells, drinking water needs RO. A lab test settles it.

    How long does a water test take?

    Chemical results take two to three working days. Bacteriological tests need about 48 hours of incubation.

    What does a maintenance contract cover?

    Scheduled visits, cartridge changes, membrane cleaning, dosing chemicals, output water checks and priority call-outs.

    Can treated sewage be reused?

    Yes. Treated STP water is widely reused for flushing, irrigation and cooling towers, cutting fresh water demand by 30–50%.

    Which areas do you cover?

    We are based in Karachi and work across Sindh, with industrial projects anywhere in Pakistan.
